Who we help

Help for the people you love, and for you

Getting a parent's home ready. Staying in your own home. Healing after a surgery. David helps with all of it.

For sons and daughters

Getting the house ready for Mom or Dad

You have a lot on your plate right now. Maybe a parent fell. Maybe they come home from rehab next week. You need someone you can trust with the house.

  • One person to call. David answers his own phone. He comes out himself and does the work himself.
  • A straight answer on price. You get a written quote before any work starts.
  • Built for your parent. Grab bars go where their hands reach. Steps match how they move.
  • Help with who pays. David does VA and insurance work. He can help you see what may apply.

Have notes from the hospital or a therapist? Share them with David. He builds to fit what the care team asked for.

Call David about your parent

For a short recovery

Coming home after surgery or an injury

The first weeks home are the hardest. A grab bar by the toilet, a rail on the steps and a safer way through the door can make them easier.

Many of these changes stay useful long after you heal. Call David before the hospital sends you home if you can. He can help you get ready.

Call David before you come home

For homeowners

Your house. Your way. For years to come.

You know every corner of this house. You want to keep living here, on your own terms. A few good changes can make that easier.

  • Get the mail and come back in without a fight with the front step.
  • Reach your pots and dishes without bending down deep or climbing on a chair.
  • Step out of the shower with a firm bar in your hand.
  • Have the grandkids over, and have room for everyone to move.

It will not look like a hospital. David is a carpenter. He builds in wood and finishes the trim so the work fits your home.

You do not need a computer for any of this. Just pick up the phone.
