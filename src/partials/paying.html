Paying for it

Help with paying for the work

Many families do not know that help may be out there. David does VA and insurance work. He can help you sort out what may apply to you.

Ways families pay

  • VA programs

    The VA has home change programs for some veterans. David does VA work. Tell him if the person in the home is a veteran.

  • Insurance

    Some insurance, such as some long-term care policies, may help pay for home changes. Ask David what he has worked with.

  • Missouri programs

    Some Missouri programs help older adults and people with disabilities make changes at home. Rules differ by program.

  • Your own savings or a loan

    Many families pay for smaller jobs themselves. You get a written quote first, so you know the price before work starts.

What helps when you call

  • Whether the person in the home is a veteran.
  • Any insurance that may cover home changes.
  • Notes from a doctor, therapist or hospital, if you have them.
  • What is hard at home right now.

Programs change, and each one has its own rules. David will help you find out what may apply. He will not promise what a program will pay.
