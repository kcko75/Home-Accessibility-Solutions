What David builds

Every house is a little different. So is every person. David measures, listens and builds for you.

  • Steps & ramps

    Built to fit your house and your yard. Solid underfoot, with rails where you need them.

  • Grab bars

    Placed for your height and your routine. Anchored firm so you can trust them.

  • Wider doorways

    Room for a walker or a wheelchair. Clean trim, so it looks like the house was built that way.

  • Accessible cabinets

    Lower counters, pull-out drawers and shelves you can reach from a chair.

How it works

No pressure and no sales pitch. Just a plain talk about what would help.

  1. Call David

    Tell him what is hard at home right now. A step, a tub, a doorway.

  2. Home visit

    David comes out, looks at the space and listens to how you use it.

  3. Written quote

    You get a clear price in writing before any work starts.

  4. Build & check

    David does the work himself, then checks back to make sure it fits.

Not sure what you need yet? An occupational therapist (OT) can look at the home and how you move in it. Your doctor can refer you. David will build from what they suggest.
