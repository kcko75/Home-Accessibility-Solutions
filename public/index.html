No remodel needed

Make the kitchen you have work better

You do not need new cabinets to reach your things. David can add smart pull-downs and pull-outs inside the cabinets you already have.

Pull-down shelves

The high cabinet comes down to you. Pull the handle and the shelves lower to counter height. No step stool.

Pull-out pantry & spice racks

Narrow racks slide out from the side of a cabinet. Everything is in front of you. Nothing is hiding in the back.

Roll-out drawers

Deep lower shelves become drawers that glide out. Pots and pans come to you, so there is less bending and kneeling.

More small changes that help

  • Lazy Susans for corner cabinets
  • Pull-out trash and recycling bins
  • Easy-grip handles in place of small knobs
  • Lever door handles in place of round knobs
  • Lower closet rods and shelves
  • Grab bars by the tub, toilet and entry doors
