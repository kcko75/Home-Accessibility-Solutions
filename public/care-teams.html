For care teams

Send your patient home to a house that is ready.

You did the hard work in therapy. David makes sure the home does not undo it.

How to refer

Call or text David at 417-496-3821. Give him the family's name and number, and your recommendations if you have them.

What David builds

  • Entry steps and ramps
  • Grab bars placed to your notes
  • Wider doorways
  • Lowered and roll-under cabinets

What you get back

David keeps the family in the loop and can send you photos of the finished work, so you know it got done.

We work with occupational and physical therapists, home health agencies, hospital discharge planners, VA staff, case managers, senior living staff, group homes, disability service providers, realtors and elder law attorneys.
