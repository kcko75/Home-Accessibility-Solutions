How it works

Simple from the first call

No pressure and no sales pitch. Just a plain talk about what would help.

  1. Call David

    Tell him what is hard at home right now. A step, a tub, a doorway.

  2. Home visit

    David comes out, looks at the space and listens to how you use it.

  3. Written quote

    You get a clear price in writing before any work starts.

  4. Build & check

    David does the work himself, then checks back to make sure it fits.

Not sure what you need yet? An occupational therapist (OT) can look at the home and how you move in it. Your doctor can refer you. David will build from what they suggest.

Common questions

Do I need to know what I need before I call?

No. Tell David what is hard at home. He will look at the space and suggest what fits. If you are not sure, an occupational therapist (OT) can also look at the home and how you move in it.

Will my house look like a hospital?

No. David is a carpenter. He builds in wood and finishes the trim so the work looks like part of your home.

Is this only for older adults?

No. David helps people of any age. That includes people with a disability and people who are healing from a surgery or an injury.

Do I need new cabinets to make my kitchen easier?

Usually not. Pull-down shelves, pull-out racks and roll-out drawers can go inside the cabinets you already have.

Can I get help paying for this?

Maybe. David does VA and insurance work. Some Missouri programs also help older adults and people with disabilities. Ask David what may apply to you.

Will you work with my therapist or care team?

Yes. Share the notes from the hospital or your therapist. David builds to fit what they asked for.

What area do you serve?

Springfield, Missouri and nearby towns. Not sure if you are in the area? Call David and ask.

Ready to make your home work for you?

Call David. Tell him what is hard right now. He will tell you what he can do.
